### Fix #5 — Business logic
**Files**: `app/Http/Controllers/Api/OrderController.php`, `app/Http/Controllers/Api/RefundController.php`

**Stock race condition**:
- `OrderController::store`: pakai `Product::whereIn(...)->lockForUpdate()->get()` di awal transaction → 2 order paralel ambil produk sama tidak race ke stock minus.
- Catatan: tidak block negative stock (V1 offline-first; report akan surface).

**Refund double-debit**:
- `RefundController::store`: tambah validation `cash_out_already_bumped` &amp; `stock_already_restored` flag dari Flutter.
- Kalau Flutter sudah local-bump (offline path), BE skip bump → tidak double.
- Kalau shift sudah `closed_at IS NOT NULL`, BE skip bump → tidak corrupt variance shift lama.
